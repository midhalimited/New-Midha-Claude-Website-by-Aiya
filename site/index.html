FAQ

Got questions?

How much does a heat pump clean cost?

Our Pro Clean is $79, Elite Clean $129 and Comprehensive Clean $189. Prices are per heat pump and include GST. Compare packages.

Do you only do test & tag for businesses?

Test & tag is mainly for workplaces, construction sites, schools, cafés and offices, but we’re happy to test gear for anyone who needs it.

Do you test medical equipment and fire extinguishers?

Yes, anywhere in New Zealand. We test medical equipment such as beds, hoists and pumps to AS/NZS 3551, and check fire extinguishers and fire blankets to NZS 4503:2005, often in the same visit as your test & tag. See medical equipment testing and fire extinguisher checks.

How quickly can you come out?

We aim to reply to every quote request within 2 hours during business hours. Wainuiomata jobs can often be booked same day or next day; see our service areas for other suburbs.

Which areas do you cover?

Test & tag is available right across New Zealand. See test & tag locations. Heat pump cleaning and electrical work cover Wainuiomata, Lower Hutt, Upper Hutt, Tawa, Newlands, Porirua and the wider Wellington region. See Wellington service areas.
