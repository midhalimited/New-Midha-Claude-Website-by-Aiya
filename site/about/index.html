About Midha Limited

Keeping your whānau, team and property safe and comfortable, with straightforward local service.

Our story

Safety, reliability and clean air.

Midha Limited started with a simple idea: every Kiwi workplace and home deserves reliable safety compliance and heating care, without red tape or hidden fees.

Based in Wainuiomata, we look after heat pumps and electrical work across Lower Hutt, Upper Hutt, Tawa, Newlands and Porirua, and test and tag workplaces right across New Zealand. We combine careful technical work with friendly, approachable service.

Midha Limited is a registered New Zealand company.
