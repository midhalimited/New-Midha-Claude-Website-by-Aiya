Giveaway terms and conditions

Last updated 21 October 2026

These terms apply to the Midha Limited “Win a free heat pump clean” giveaway. By entering, you agree to them.

Promoter

The promoter is Midha Limited, Wainuiomata, Lower Hutt. Contact midhalimited@gmail.com or 022 407 8603.

The prize

  • Each draw has one prize: a free Pro Clean (valued at $79 including GST) for one heat pump at a home in the Wellington region.
  • The prize can’t be exchanged for cash or transferred to someone else.
  • The Pro Clean must be booked within 3 months of the draw, at a time that suits both the winner and Midha Limited.

Who can enter

  • You must be 18 or older and live in the Wellington region, with a heat pump at your home in the region.
  • Midha Limited staff, partner technicians and their immediate families can’t enter.
  • One entry per person per draw.

How to enter

Entry is free and you don’t need to buy anything. To enter, follow the steps in our giveaway post on Facebook during the draw period.

Draw periods and draws

  • The giveaway runs in 3-month periods ending on 31 March, 30 June, 30 September and 31 December.
  • The winner for each period is drawn at random from all valid entries received during that period. The draw takes place within 5 working days after the period ends.
  • Entries don’t carry over to the next period.

Winners

  • We’ll contact the winner through Facebook. If the winner doesn’t reply within 7 days, or isn’t eligible, we’ll draw another winner.
  • With the winner’s permission, we may share their first name and suburb on our website or Facebook page.

Your information

We only use entrants’ information to run the giveaway and contact the winner. See our privacy policy.
