Why medical equipment is different

Normal test & tag doesn’t cover medical devices.

A normal AS/NZS 3760:2022 tag doesn’t count for medical equipment. Under the Electricity (Safety) Regulations 2010, the owner of an electrical medical device must have it checked regularly to AS/NZS 3551, which has tighter safety limits.

We test your medical equipment to AS/NZS 3551 using a tester made for the job, and test everything else in the building to AS/NZS 3760:2022 in the same visit.

  • Visual inspection of each device, its cord and plug
  • Protective earth, insulation and leakage current tests to AS/NZS 3551
  • A label on each device showing the test date, next due date, tests done and the standard
  • An asset register and report you can show at your accreditation audit
  • Faulty items clearly tagged out of service and listed in your report
  • Normal test & tag of your other appliances in the same visit

FAQ

Medical equipment testing questions

Is medical equipment testing a legal requirement?

Yes. Under regulation 91 of the Electricity (Safety) Regulations 2010, the owner of an electrical medical device must make sure it is checked regularly to AS/NZS 3551. Using a device that hasn’t been checked is an offence, except in an emergency. The person who tests it gives you a record of the results.

How often does medical equipment need testing?

At least every 12 months, or more often if the manufacturer says so. New equipment should also be tested before it’s first used, and after any repair.

Isn’t normal test & tag enough?

No. A normal AS/NZS 3760:2022 tag doesn’t count for medical devices, because medical equipment has tighter safety limits. Your other appliances, like kettles, computers and staff fridges, still get normal test & tag, and we do both in one visit.

Can you test without disrupting patients?

Yes. We work room by room around your clinic hours, or before opening and after closing.

Do you test patient monitors, ECGs or infusion pumps?

No. These need specialist biomedical testing. We’re happy to suggest a biomedical engineer, and we can test the rest of your equipment.

Will the report help with our audit?

Yes. You get a register listing every device with its result, test date and next due date, which you can show your auditor.
