What’s included

Everything you need for compliance.

FAQ

Test & tag questions

How often does equipment need testing?

It depends on the environment. Construction and hire equipment is usually tested every 3 months, factories and workshops every 6 months, and offices and other low-risk places every 12 months or longer. We’ll recommend the right interval for your site.

How much does test & tag cost?

It depends on how many items you have and where they are. Tell us roughly how many items and we’ll send a fixed-price quote, usually within 2 hours.

Do you provide certificates for audits?

Yes. You’ll receive a full digital PDF register of every item tested, usually within 24 hours of the job.

How does the QR tagging work?

Each item gets a tag with a unique QR code. Scan it with a phone to see when it was tested, the result and when the next test is due.

Do you test RCDs (safety switches)?

Yes. We test portable and fixed RCDs for trip time and push-button operation, and record the results in your register.
