FAQ

Test & tag in Christchurch: questions

Do you test hire equipment?

Yes. Hire equipment is usually tested every 3 months, with a visual check before each hire. We can set up a regular schedule for your fleet.

Can you tag our equipment before we open?

Yes. We can check and tag your equipment before opening day, so your register is complete from the start.

How often does equipment need testing?

It depends on the environment. Construction and hire equipment is usually tested every 3 months, factories and workshops every 6 months, and offices and other low-risk places every 12 months or longer. We’ll recommend the right interval for your site.

How much does test & tag cost?

It depends on how many items you have and where they are. Tell us roughly how many items and where your site is, and we’ll send a fixed-price quote that includes any travel, usually within 2 hours.
