Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Midha Limited (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who we share it with, and your rights under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Who we are

Midha Limited is a New Zealand company based in Wainuiomata, Lower Hutt. We provide test & tag, medical equipment testing and fire extinguisher checks across New Zealand, and heat pump cleaning and electrical services in the Wellington region. For anything about your personal information, contact our privacy officer at midhalimited@gmail.com or 022 407 8603.

What we collect

  • When you ask for a quote or book a job: your name, phone number, email, suburb or town, the service you need and any job details you give us.
  • When we do the job: the site address, an on-site contact person, access details, and job records such as test & tag, medical equipment and fire equipment registers, test results, photos of the equipment or heat pump, and invoices. We don’t need or collect patient information when testing medical equipment.
  • When you call, text or email us: your message and contact details.
  • When you enter our giveaway: your Facebook name and what you post on the giveaway post. If you win, we ask for your contact details and the address of the heat pump to be cleaned.
  • When you visit our website: basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, which our hosting provider uses to keep the site running and secure.

You don’t have to give us your details, but without a phone number or email we can’t reply to you, and without a site address we can’t do the job.

Information we get from other people

Sometimes we get your details from someone else, such as your employer or site manager when they book test & tag, a landlord or property manager who books a heat pump clean and gives us a tenant’s contact for access, a person who refers you to us, or Facebook when you enter our giveaway. We only use these details to arrange and do the job, or to run the giveaway. When we contact you, we’ll tell you who we are and why we have your details, unless you already know.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, quote, book and carry out the job.
  • To prepare test & tag registers and remind you when retests are due.
  • To invoice you and keep business and tax records.
  • To run the giveaway and contact the winner.
  • To keep our website working and secure, and to see which of our Google ads lead to enquiries.

We won’t send you marketing emails or texts unless you’ve agreed to them, and every marketing message will let you unsubscribe.

Who we share it with

We never sell your personal information. We only share it when we need to:

  • Partner technicians who carry out test & tag jobs for us outside the Wellington region. They get only what they need to do your job, such as the site address, contact person and equipment details.
  • Partners we arrange work through, such as a registered electrician for new electrical installations, or a specialist who refills and pressure tests fire extinguishers. They also get only what they need to do the work.
  • Service providers who help us run the business: our email provider (Google), our website host (Cloudflare), our website form provider (Formspree), our font provider (Fontshare), and our accountant. Because we advertise on Google, Google also receives information about ad clicks, as explained below.
  • Meta (Facebook), which runs the platform our giveaway takes place on.
  • Anyone we’re required to share it with by law.

Information stored overseas

Some of our service providers store information outside New Zealand, mainly in the United States. We use well-known providers with strong security, and we only use them to store and process information for us, not for their own purposes.

Cookies and advertising

Our website doesn’t set its own tracking cookies. We advertise on Google, so our website uses Google’s advertising tag. It sets cookies that tell us when someone who clicked one of our ads sends a quote request or taps to call us, so we know which ads are useful. It doesn’t give us your name or contact details. You can manage Google ad settings at myadcenter.google.com.

How we keep it safe

We keep personal information in password-protected accounts, limit who can see it, and only keep it as long as we need it. Job and invoice records are kept for at least 7 years, because New Zealand tax law requires it. Quote enquiries that don’t go ahead are deleted when we no longer need them.

If a privacy breach happens that is likely to cause serious harm, we’ll tell the people affected and the Privacy Commissioner as soon as we can.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it’s wrong. Contact us at midhalimited@gmail.com or 022 407 8603. We’ll respond within 20 working days and won’t charge you to see your information.

If you’re not happy with how we’ve handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz or 0800 803 909.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
