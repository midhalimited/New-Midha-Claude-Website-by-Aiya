FAQ

Heat pump questions

Are your prices per heat pump?

Yes. All package prices are per heat pump and include GST. For two heat pumps on the Elite Clean, the total is $258.

Which package should I choose?

If your heat pump has been cleaned in the last year, Pro Clean is usually enough. Elite Clean is our most popular choice for a yearly service. Choose Comprehensive if you can smell mould or see black spots on the fan.

Why does my heat pump smell musty?

Mould and bacteria grow on the damp coils and fan inside the indoor unit. A filter wipe won’t reach them. Our Elite and Comprehensive cleans wash them out, and Comprehensive adds an anti-mould treatment with a 3-month guarantee.

Why is my heat pump blowing cold air or using more power?

Blocked filters, dirty coils and leaves in the outdoor unit make your heat pump work harder. A clean usually restores airflow and efficiency. If it still isn’t right, we’ll check for faults.

What does the 3-month mould-free guarantee cover?

If mould comes back within 3 months of a Comprehensive Clean, we’ll come back and re-clean the unit for free.

How long does a clean take?

Allow 1 to 2 hours per heat pump depending on the package and access. We’ll give you a time window when you book.
