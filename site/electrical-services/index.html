FAQ

Electrical questions

Do you do small jobs?

Yes. Swapping a light fitting or adding a power point is exactly the kind of job we’re happy to take on.

Can you switch my old downlights to LED?

Yes. LED downlights use far less power and run cooler than halogens. We’ll quote for the number of lights you want changed.
